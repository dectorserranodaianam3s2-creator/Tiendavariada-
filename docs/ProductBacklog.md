# 🛒 Product Backlog – Online Store (Variety Shop)

## 📌 Product Owner
**Product Owner:** Daiana Serrano  
**Project:** Online Store – Variety Shop  
**Objective:** Create an online store that offers a simple, secure, and attractive shopping experience for users on both mobile and desktop devices.

---

# 🎯 Epic 1: First Impression & Trust
**Goal:** Users quickly understand and trust the store.

## User Stories

### US01
**As a user, I want to clearly see what the store sells when I enter, so I can decide if I am interested.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- The homepage displays featured products.
- The store category is visible in the first section.
- Users can identify the type of products within 5 seconds.

---

### US02
**As a user, I want to see a clear value proposition, so I understand what makes the store different.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- The homepage includes a short store description.
- Promotions or unique benefits are highlighted.
- The value proposition is visible without scrolling too much.

---

### US03
**As a user, I want to see trust signals (reviews, social media, contact info), so I feel safe buying.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Social media links are visible.
- Contact information is easy to find.
- Customer reviews are displayed on the homepage or product pages.

**Priority:** High

---

# 🗂️ Epic 2: Catalog & Navigation
**Goal:** Help users find products easily.

## User Stories

### US04
**As a user, I want to see clear categories, so I can browse easily.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Categories are displayed in the navigation menu.
- Categories are organized clearly.
- Users can access categories with one click.

---

### US05
**As a user, I want to use filters (price, size, color), so I can quickly find what I need.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Filters are available on catalog pages.
- Products update according to selected filters.
- Users can remove filters easily.

---

### US06
**As a user, I want a functional search bar, so I can find specific products.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- The search bar is visible on all pages.
- Search results appear in less than 3 seconds.
- Relevant products are displayed based on keywords.

**Priority:** High

---

# 📸 Epic 3: Product Visualization
**Goal:** Build trust through visuals.

## User Stories

### US07
**As a user, I want to see high-quality images, so I can evaluate the product.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Product images are clear and high resolution.
- Images load correctly on mobile and desktop.
- Each product has at least one image.

---

### US08
**As a user, I want to see multiple angles of the product, so I can understand it better.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Products include at least 3 images from different angles.
- Users can zoom in on images.
- Images can be viewed in gallery mode.

---

### US09
**As a user, I want to see real-life or in-use photos, so I can trust the purchase.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Some products include lifestyle images.
- Customer-uploaded images can be displayed.
- Images represent the real product accurately.

**Priority:** High

---

# 📝 Epic 4: Product Information
**Goal:** Reduce doubts before purchase.

## User Stories

### US10
**As a user, I want to see complete product descriptions (material, size, etc.), so I can make informed decisions.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Product descriptions include size, material, and specifications.
- Information is displayed clearly.
- Descriptions are available for all products.

---

### US11
**As a user, I want to know what is included in the product, so I avoid confusion.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Included items are listed clearly.
- Product packaging details are specified.
- Missing accessories are clarified.

**Priority:** High

---

# 💸 Epic 5: Pricing & Transparency
**Goal:** Avoid surprises.

## User Stories

### US12
**As a user, I want to see the full price upfront, so I avoid hidden costs.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Product prices are visible before checkout.
- Taxes or extra fees are specified.
- Prices are displayed in local currency.

---

### US13
**As a user, I want to see clear promotions, so I can take advantage of offers.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Discounts are highlighted visually.
- Original and discounted prices are shown.
- Promotion validity dates are displayed.

**Priority:** High

---

# 🚚 Epic 6: Shipping

## User Stories

### US14

### Acceptance Criteria
- Shipping costs are calculated before payment.

---

### US15

### Acceptance Criteria

## User Stories

### US16

### Acceptance Criteria

---

### US17

### Acceptance Criteria

# ⭐ Epic 8: Reviews & Social Proof
**Goal:** Build trust through other users.

## User Stories

### US18
**As a user, I want to see customer reviews, so I can trust the product.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Reviews are visible on product pages.
- Ratings use a star system.
- Users can sort reviews by relevance or date.

---

### US19
**As a user, I want to see customer photos, so I can validate quality.**

---

# 📱 Epic 9: Design & Performance
**Goal:** Provide a smooth experience, especially on mobile devices.

## User Stories

### US20
**As a user, I want the store to work well on my phone, so I can shop easily.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- The design adapts to mobile screens.
- Buttons and menus are easy to use on touch devices.
- Mobile navigation is responsive.

---

### US21
**As a user, I want fast loading times, so I don’t waste time.**

### Acceptance Criteria
- Pages load in less than 3 seconds.
- Images are optimized for performance.
- The website performs correctly on different devices.

**Priority:** High
