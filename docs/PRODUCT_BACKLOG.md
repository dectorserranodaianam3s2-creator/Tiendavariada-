 🛒PRODUCT BACKLOG – ONLINE STORE (VARIETY SHOP)

📌 Product Vision

Develop an online variety store where users can easily browse, search, and purchase products from different categories in a secure and user-friendly platform.


---

🎯 Product Goal

Provide customers with an easy, fast, and secure online shopping experience where they can explore different product categories, make purchases, leave reviews, and choose convenient payment methods.


---

👥 Stakeholders

Stakeholder	Interest in the Project

Customers	Buy products easily and securely
Store Owner	Increase sales and manage products efficiently
Development Team	Build and maintain the platform
Product Owner	Ensure the project meets customer needs
Scrum Master	Organize teamwork and Scrum process



---

🧩 Epics

Epic	Description

Customers	Users can create accounts and manage their profiles
